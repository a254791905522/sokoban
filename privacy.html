Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. No Data Collection

Sokoban - Fantasy Jelly Factory is a fully offline single-player puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game operates entirely on your device without requiring any network connection.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • No device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising ID)
  • No location data or geolocation tracking
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No contacts, calendar, or media library access
  • No camera or microphone access

3. Local Data Storage

Your game progress, level completion status, and in-app settings are stored locally on your device using iOS's standard local storage mechanisms. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

4. No Third-Party Services

The game does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, social media services, or other external integrations. There is no data sharing with any third party.

5. No In-App Purchases or Advertisements

The game does not contain any in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertisements. There are no payment transactions of any kind.

6. Children's Privacy

Since the game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

All locally stored game data is protected by iOS device-level security features, including hardware encryption on supported devices. We rely on Apple's built-in security model to protect your local data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Since the game operates entirely offline, you are encouraged to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store page for this app.
