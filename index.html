Sokoban

Fantasy Jelly Factory — Push Boxes, Solve Puzzles, Collect Stars

About the Game

Sokoban - Fantasy Jelly Factory is a classic push-box puzzle game set in a whimsical jelly factory. Guide your worker through challenging levels, push crates and boxes into position, and collect stars to unlock new stages!

Simple to learn, impossible to put down. Every level presents a unique spatial puzzle — plan your moves carefully, think ahead, and use the undo button when you need a second chance.

How to Play

  • Tap directional buttons to move your character up, down, left, or right
  • Push boxes and crates onto designated target spots to complete each level
  • Collect all 3 stars in each level for a perfect score
  • Use the undo button to reverse mistakes
  • Unlock new levels as you progress through the factory

Features

  • Classic Sokoban puzzle gameplay with a charming jelly factory theme
  • Multiple levels with increasing difficulty
  • 3-star rating system per level
  • Undo moves to correct mistakes
  • Beautiful, colorful art style
  • Play completely offline — no internet connection needed
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no distractions
